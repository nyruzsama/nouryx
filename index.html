Calamba Heritage & Community

WHY THIRD SPACES MATTER

"First is your home. Second is your workplace. The third is where your soul unwinds."

Nestled right beside the historic Bahay ni Rizal in Poblacion, Calamba City, Grind. Coffee was built with a clear purpose: to be more than just a place to buy a drink.

We are a sanctuary where students study in peace, creators find inspiration, friends catch up after long days, and travelers rest in the heart of Dr. Jose Rizal's hometown. Every cup is brewed with locally inspired recipes — from unrefined muscovado sugar to premium matcha and fresh artisan breads.

Whether you stay for five minutes or five hours, our doors are always open to welcome you home.
