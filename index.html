About Hungry Pair

Crafted with Passion, Served with Big Portions in Calamba, Laguna.

Welcome to Hungry Pair! Located on Burgos Street in Calamba, we specialize in delivering overflowing seafood feasts and comforting Japanese meals that never skimp on flavor or quantity.

Our signature Seafood Buckets are drenched in authentic Cajun and garlic-butter aromatics, brimming with whole crabs, succulent prawns, plump scallops, mussels, and squid. Alongside our Japanese Gyudon beef bowls, crispy Tebasaki wings, and dessert waffles, we guarantee a feast you will love.
