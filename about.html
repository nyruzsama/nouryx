The Hungry Pair Story

How a passion for fresh ocean flavors and warm Filipino gatherings turned into Calamba's favorite seafood destination.

One PAIR at a Time

Hungry Pair was founded on a simple Filipino truth: the best memories happen around a shared table with warm food and the people you cherish.

Located on Burgos Street in Calamba, Laguna, we created a welcoming dining sanctuary where diners can leave the rush of the day behind and immerse themselves in the comforting aroma of sizzling garlic butter, smoky charcoal grills, and freshly steamed catch.

Whether you're pairing grilled bangus with savory garlic rice, or pairing your lunch break with a best friend, Hungry Pair is where great moments begin.

Crafted with Pride & Freshness

We believe great seafood demands two things: uncompromising freshness and honest preparation.

Every morning, our team carefully selects seafood directly to ensure that each crab, squid, prawn, and fish that enters our kitchen is of prime quality. We season with native calamansi, garlic, local chilies, and heritage marinades before grilling over natural coconut charcoal.
